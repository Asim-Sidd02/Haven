# Haven — Brand mark

## The idea: "The Door"
A bouncer's job is the entrance: two stanchions with a velvet rope between them. Haven's mark draws that scene with as few shapes as possible:

- **Two figures**: the guards at the door, or two people standing together.
- **Together they form an H**, for *Haven*.
- **The velvet rope dips into a gentle smile**, for the *care* in "Safety, booked with care".
- **The shield** holds it all: protection and a safe place.

## Type
Wordmark: **Lora SemiBold**. Tagline: **Nunito Sans**, uppercase, wide letter-spacing.

## Do / Don't
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the width of one figure.
- Use it at 16px or larger (it stays legible down to that size).
- Don't rotate it, add outlines or shadows, or change the rope's colour to anything outside the palette.
